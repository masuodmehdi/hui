Managing Messages

You can view received, sent, or draft messages.

Context

  • : saves messages received.
  • : saves messages sent, including messages that have been sent successfully and messages that failed to be sent.
  • : saves draft messages.

Procedure

Expand All
  1. Choose > //.
  2. From the displayed page,

    • Turn pages and view messages.
    • Refresh the storage status.
    • Mark or unmark messages.
    • Delete marked messages.

  3. Click a message to view the message details. Then you can:

    • Reply to the message.
    • Forward the message.
    • Delete the message.
