Configuring the SMS

You can set SMS Center number and turn on SMS receiving reports function. With SMS center number right configured, then your USIM card can send and receive messages. The SMS center number is provided by the USIM card service provider.

Procedure

  1. Choose > .
  2. Set SMS parameters.
    • Set the SMS center number.
      NOTE:

      The number is updated automatically after a USIM card is inserted. If the number is left blank or deleted, please contact the USIM card service provider.

    • Turn on or off the function of receiving reports. If the receiving reports function is turned on, after each message you have send, you will receive a report which indicating that messages have been sent successful or not.
  3. Click to save the settings.
